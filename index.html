Scene 01 / The opening shot

Of all the places I could have been, somehow, I found myself happiest beside you.

Baby Julie, I love how easy it is to be myself around you. I can tell you whatever’s on my mind or sit with you quietly and feel just as close. Somewhere along the way, being beside you became the part of my day I look forward to most.

Scene 02

Today’s little forever.

Baby Julie, I was so happy spending time with you today. I keep going back to the little things. The look on your face when something made you smile. The way one thought turned into another until I forgot what we were talking about in the first place. Even the quiet moments felt easy with you there.

I wish I could remember every little thing we said. What I do remember is how often I caught myself thinking, I’m really happy right here. It feels a little silly to miss you this soon, but I do. If I could have asked today for anything more, it would’ve been a few extra minutes beside you.

Scene 03 / The distance after goodbye

“Funny how a beautiful day can end, but the feeling of having you beside me stays.”

My Baby Julie,

I thought I’d get home and do the usual things, but I keep pausing in the middle of them because something reminds me of you. I miss looking over and seeing your smile. I miss knowing that if a thought came into my head, I could just turn to you and say it. It’s funny how much I notice those little things once I’m home and the room gets quiet.

If I could go back to any part of today, I think I’d pick a moment when neither of us was in a hurry. I’d sit there a little longer and let you finish whatever you were telling me. Then I’d tell you how glad I was to be there. I thought it more than once today. I wish I had said it every time.

Tonight, I’m thanking God that He let our paths meet. There was a time when I had no idea who you were, and now you’re the person I want to tell first when something good happens. I’m grateful I get to know you slowly, little by little, and see the sides of you that come out when you feel comfortable.

I’m already looking forward to the next time I see you. I’ll probably have something silly to tell you by then. Before I get to any of it, I think I’ll just be happy to see your face again.

With love, James

Scene 05

My promise to you.

Baby Julie, I don’t need every day with you to be perfect. I want the ordinary ones, too. I want to keep making memories with you and learning what matters to you, even when the answer changes. When you tell me about a dream of yours, I want to remember it and ask how it’s going. And when a day feels heavy, I want to listen and ask what would help.

Ad astra. To the stars. I hope you go after the things that make your eyes light up when you talk about them. I’d love to be there cheering you on.

In omnibus, gratias ago tibi. In everything, I thank you. Thank you for making room for me in your days and for the kindness and love you show me in small ways. I want you to hear how much I appreciate you on ordinary days, too.

Semper te amabo. I will always love you.

THE END?

Maybe the movie ends here, Baby Julie, but my favorite story is the one I get to keep writing with you.

Thank you for today. Thank you for being part of my happiest memories. And if you ever wonder whether I miss you after we say goodbye, the answer is always yes.

Until our next scene, my Baby Julie.
